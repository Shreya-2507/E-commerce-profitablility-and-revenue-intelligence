# 📊 E-Commerce Profitability & Revenue Intelligence

### Analyzing Sales, Revenue, Profitability, and Discount Strategies Using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an E-Commerce Superstore dataset to understand business performance through sales, revenue, profit, and discount analysis.

The primary objective is to identify products and sub-categories that generate high sales but contribute relatively low profits, investigate the impact of discounts on profitability, and provide actionable business recommendations to improve overall financial performance.

Using SQL, the project explores business performance across different product categories and sub-categories to uncover profitability risks and revenue opportunities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, revenue, and profit performance.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Detect loss-making sub-categories and investigate their financial performance.
- Evaluate the relationship between discount rates and profit margins.
- Identify high-revenue areas with low profitability.
- Develop data-driven recommendations for improving business profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **SQL:** Data querying, aggregation, filtering, and business analysis.
- **SQL Database:** Superstore dataset.
- **Git & GitHub:** Project documentation and version control.

## 📂 Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales & Revenue Analysis
- Evaluated total revenue across product categories and sub-categories.
- Compared sales performance to identify high-revenue segments.
- Analyzed quantity sold to understand product demand.

### 2. Profitability Analysis
- Calculated total profit and profit margins.
- Identified profitable and loss-making sub-categories.
- Compared revenue generation with actual profitability.

### 3. Discount Impact Analysis
- Examined average discount percentages across sub-categories.
- Investigated how discounting relates to profit margins.
- Identified areas where high discounts may be contributing to profitability risks.

### 4. Business Performance Analysis
- Compared revenue, quantity, profit margins, and average discounts.
- Identified opportunities for improving pricing and discount strategies.
- Developed recommendations based on observed business performance.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several important findings:

- **Chairs:** Generated strong sales within the Furniture category, with an average discount of approximately 17% and a profit margin of 8.10%.
- **Bookcases:** Experienced negative profitability alongside an average discount of approximately 21%, indicating a potential discount-management issue.
- **Tables:** Generated relatively high sales quantities but remained loss-making, suggesting an opportunity to review pricing and discount strategies.
- **Furnishings:** Maintained positive profitability, although discount adjustments could potentially improve margins.
- **Binders:** Showed an opportunity to investigate whether reducing discounts could improve profitability.
- **Supplies:** Experienced losses despite relatively low average discounts, indicating that discounting alone may not explain its poor performance.
- **Art:** Demonstrated comparatively healthy business performance.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Review discount strategies for loss-making sub-categories, particularly Bookcases and Tables.
2. Experiment with gradual discount reductions instead of immediately eliminating discounts.
3. Monitor sales volume and profit margins together when evaluating pricing changes.
4. Investigate operational costs and other expenses to understand the reasons behind losses.
5. Prioritize business segments that generate sustainable profits rather than focusing exclusively on revenue growth.

## 📈 Key Performance Metrics

The project focuses on the following business metrics:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Measures overall sales performance |
| Total Profit | Measures financial gains |
| Profit Margin (%) | Evaluates profitability relative to revenue |
| Average Discount (%) | Measures discounting patterns |
| Total Quantity Sold | Measures product demand |

**Profit Margin Formula:**

`(Total Profit / Total Sales) × 100`

**Average Discount Formula:**

`AVG(Discount) × 100`

## ⚠️ Project Limitations

- The available dataset contains product categories and sub-categories rather than individual product names. Therefore, the analysis focuses on sub-category-level performance.
- The dataset does not provide sufficient detailed expense information to calculate complete product-level operational costs.
- The findings represent historical dataset patterns and should be validated through further business experiments before implementing pricing changes.

## 🏁 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw e-commerce sales data into meaningful business insights.

Rather than focusing only on revenue and sales volume, the analysis highlights the importance of understanding profit margins, discount strategies, and loss-making business segments.

The ultimate goal is to help businesses make informed decisions that balance revenue growth with sustainable profitability.
